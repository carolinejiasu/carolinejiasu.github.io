Second Order: AI and the Future of the Workforce

A survey and interview project exploring how AI may reshape jobs, education, regulation, and human skills.

As someone who will enter the workforce in a few years, I wanted to make this uncertain subject more clear and understandable. I surveyed high school students, college students, and professionals about how AI is affecting their fields.

I received and analyzed over 100 responses and synthesized my findings into a report comparing perceptions of AI’s impact on the labor market, potential applications, and appropriate risk governance frameworks.

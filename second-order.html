Second Order: AI and the Future of the Workforce

A survey and interview project exploring how AI may reshape jobs, education, regulation, and human skills.

As a student who will enter the workforce in a few years, I wanted to make an uncertain subject more concrete. I surveyed high school students, college students, and professionals about how AI is affecting their fields.

I aggregated approximately 100 responses into a data report comparing attitudes toward AI’s risks, most useful applications, and appropriate regulation.

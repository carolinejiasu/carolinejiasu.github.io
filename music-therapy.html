Music Therapy

The Annotated Music Knowledge Base is designed to help music therapists identify listening material efficiently.

After more than a decade as a musician, I became interested in music therapy: a clinical, evidence-based practice in which credentialed therapists use musical interventions to pursue non-musical health goals.

Through Hopeful Minds Outreach, I connected with music-therapy organizations and learned that access is constrained by limited resources. That led me to develop the Annotated Music Knowledge Base, a database that categorizes music by therapy-relevant features.

The project’s goal is to simplify the process of identifying music that may be appropriate for individual clients.
